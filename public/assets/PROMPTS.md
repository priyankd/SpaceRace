# Game sprite prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The user-supplied asteroid image was a material and lighting reference. Original generated PNGs are retained; the game clips their silhouettes during rendering to exclude the generated backdrops. Background-extraction retries did not produce usable alpha for every asset and are not used.

Final assets: `supercar-rear.png`, `asteroid-round.png`, `asteroid-long.png`, `asteroid-angular.png`.

## car

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG sprite for a chase-camera racing game. Render one Lamborghini Aventador-style lime green supercar, viewed DIRECTLY FROM BEHIND and slightly above, perfectly symmetrical centered rear view, showing low wedge body, broad rear haunches, angular black engine cover, rear window, rear diffuser, two narrow red Y-shaped taillights, black wheels tucked into body, and a wide black rear wing. Nose points away toward TOP of image, rear bumper closest at BOTTOM. Realistic high quality 3D racing game render with crisp reflections and believable metallic lime paint. Complete car visible, fills 85% of image width, approximately 1.5 times as wide as its visible height. Single car only, no perspective turn to left or right, no floor, no cast ground shadow, no scenery, no flames, no UI, no text. GENUINELY TRANSPARENT background, clean alpha cutout, no checkerboard.
